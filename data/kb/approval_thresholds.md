# Procurement Approval Thresholds

## Purpose and scope
This policy defines who must approve a contract renewal, a renegotiation, or a new
vendor engagement at Zensar BizOps. It applies to all software licences, annual
maintenance contracts (AMC), managed service agreements, and subcontractor
statements of work. It does not apply to employee payroll or statutory payments.

## Annual value bands
Approval authority is determined by the Total Contract Value (TCV) for one renewal
term, not by the monthly run rate. Convert monthly contracts by multiplying by the
number of months in the renewal term.

Band A covers contracts under 10,00,000 INR per year. The BizOps Manager may approve
these directly. No finance review is required.

Band B covers contracts from 10,00,000 up to 50,00,000 INR per year. These require
BizOps Manager approval plus a documented Finance Business Partner review.

Band C covers contracts above 50,00,000 INR per year. These require Procurement Head
approval and cannot be auto-renewed under any circumstances. A competitive quote from
at least one alternative vendor must be attached to the approval request.

## Human approval is mandatory
No contract in Band B or Band C may be renewed, terminated, or renegotiated on the
recommendation of an automated system alone. Automated analysis may produce a
recommendation and a supporting rationale, but a named human approver must record
the final decision before any commitment is made to the vendor.

## Delegation during absence
Approval authority may be delegated in writing for a maximum of 30 days. Delegation
does not change the value band. A delegate approving a Band C contract still requires
the Procurement Head's countersignature within five working days.
